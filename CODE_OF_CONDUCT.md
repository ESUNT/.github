# ESUNT Community Code of Conduct

ESUNT welcomes constructive collaboration among developers, researchers, designers, testers, and other contributors.

This code of conduct applies to ESUNT organization repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other participants with respect and professionalism.
- Offer constructive technical criticism focused on ideas, evidence, and implementation.
- Be considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and confidential information.
- Help maintain a productive environment for technical collaboration.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption, spam, and publication of another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

Do not post confidential project materials or sensitive security vulnerabilities in public discussions. Report security concerns using our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting a concern

Send a private message to **contact@esunt.com** with the subject **ESUNT Community Conduct Report**. Include relevant links and a concise description. Avoid forwarding unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Project maintainers will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate actions. Reports will be handled with discretion, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

## Scope

These expectations apply to everyone participating in ESUNT-managed community spaces, including maintainers and collaborators.
